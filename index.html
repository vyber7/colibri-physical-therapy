Good to know

Common questions.

Do I need a referral?

In many states, you can see a physical therapist without a physician referral. This answer will be updated for the practice’s location and local requirements.

Do you accept insurance?

We offer straightforward self-pay pricing and can provide a superbill for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Final billing details will be added before launch.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Your first visit typically includes a conversation about your goals, a movement assessment, an explanation of findings, and the beginning of treatment.

Do you offer virtual appointments?

Yes. Virtual visits may be available for movement coaching, exercise progression, and follow-up care.
