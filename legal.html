Privacy Policy

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions. You may also opt out of nonessential communications at any time. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. To make a request, use the contact information below; we may need to verify your identity.

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through the website without authorization from a parent or guardian.
